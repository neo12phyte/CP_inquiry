Semester Assessment Framework

A feasible, portfolio-led school grading approach for Personal and Professional Skills (PPS): four semesters, 100% each, with no traditional written examination.

Assessment componentWeight

Evidence over examinations

Use inquiry products, applied tasks, learning artefacts, portfolio reviews and presentations as evidence of learning.

Gradual independence

Make portfolio quality and students' ability to select and explain evidence more important in the second year.

Manageable marking

Assess clear milestone submissions rather than scoring every weekly activity. Use common rubrics and brief check-ins.

Important distinction

These are suggested internal school percentages, not an IB-prescribed PPS grading formula. CP core completion requirements and school reporting grades should be considered separately.

Framework developed for the PPS / Fieldnotes two-year plan. Align each component with the relevant inquiry objectives, school reporting policy and portfolio evidence. Avoid double-counting an artefact under two categories.
